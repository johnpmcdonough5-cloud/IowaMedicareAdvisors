About Our Office

Independent guidance, not a call center.

We are an independent Medicare office based in Oelwein, Iowa, working with clients nationwide. Our job is to explain your options clearly and help you choose coverage that fits your health needs and budget.

Who we are

Iowa Medicare Advisors works directly with people who are turning 65, retiring, or reevaluating their current Medicare coverage. We are licensed to discuss Medicare Advantage and related plan options and work with a range of carriers, which allows us to compare plans rather than represent a single company.

We keep our approach simple: ask questions about your doctors, prescriptions, and budget, explain the plans available where you live, and let you decide. There is no cost to meet with us, and no obligation to enroll.

Our approach

We explain, we don't pressure.
Medicare decisions matter. We would rather you take time to understand your options than rush a decision.
We work for you, not one insurance company.
Because we compare multiple carriers, our recommendations are based on fit, not a single company's product line.
We stay available after enrollment.
Questions about a claim, a pharmacy, or a renewal don't stop once you're enrolled — neither does our support.
