Who we are

Iowa Medicare Advisors is an independent Medicare office in Oelwein. We work with Iowans who are turning 65, retiring, or taking another look at the coverage they already have.

Because we compare plans from several carriers instead of representing one company, we can tell you plainly which plan fits your doctors, your prescriptions, and your budget. There is no cost to meet with us, and no obligation to enroll.

We also stay available after you enroll. Questions about a claim, a pharmacy, or next year's renewal do not stop once the paperwork is done — neither do we.

How we help

Medicare has a lot of moving parts. Our role is to explain them plainly and help you compare options based on your coverage needs, doctors, and prescriptions — not sales pressure.

Plan Comparison

We review Medicare Advantage and related plan options available in your county and compare costs, networks, and coverage side by side.

Enrollment Support

We walk you through enrollment periods and deadlines, and help you complete enrollment correctly and on time.

Ongoing Assistance

Coverage needs change. We're available year-round to answer questions and review your plan during renewal season.
